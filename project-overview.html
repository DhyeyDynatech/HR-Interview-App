AI that interviews candidates
so your recruiters don't have to

HR Interviewer conducts live, voice-driven AI interviews with candidates, scores their responses and resumes automatically, and surfaces ranked candidates to your hiring team — all without a human on the other end of the call.

The Problem

Initial screening interviews are a bottleneck. Recruiters spend hours on 30-minute calls that could be automated, introducing human bias, inconsistency, and high per-hire cost. Resume review is manual and slow. Key hiring signal disappears in unstructured notes.

The Solution

An AI agent runs every first-round interview by voice — asking consistent, structured questions, following up intelligently, detecting cheating, recording everything, and scoring both the conversation and the resume automatically. Recruiters see only ranked, analyzed candidates.
